## Design Context

### Users
Thai medical records and coding staff reviewing DRG estimates before claims.

### Brand Personality
Readable, restrained, professional. Preserve the green identity.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light surfaces, dark text, generous field widths and ordered sections. The user confirmed this direction and AN or patient-name search with admission selection.

### Design Principles
- General information comes first in both visual and keyboard order.
- Keep diagnosis names readable at desktop and mobile widths.
- Select a specific admission before importing patient data.
- Preserve calculation behavior and clearly label estimates.
